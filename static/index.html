Lakshya Setup & Mobile Browser Guide

Step-by-step instructions for Mobile Browser, PWA install, and Remote Access

🌐 1. Accessing on Mobile Browser

Open Google Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone. Type your server address in the browser bar. No heavy third-party app download is required.

📲 2. Install as Native App (1-Tap PWA)

🤖 Android (Google Chrome)

Android (Chrome): Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top-right corner -> Select 'Install app' or 'Add to Home screen'. A full-screen app icon will be pinned to your phone!

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Apple Safari)

iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button (⎋) at the bottom -> Scroll down and tap 'Add to Home Screen (+)' -> Tap 'Add'.

⚡ 3. Ultra-Fast Offline Caching

Lakshya operates with full service worker caching and offline SQLite persistence, opening in under 100ms even with patchy internet.

🏠 At Home (Home Wi-Fi)

Local LAN

When connected to your home Wi-Fi network, open directly at:

http://192.168.29.2:8856

🚀 Outside / Travel (Mobile Data & Tailscale)

Remote VPN

When away from home, connect securely using Tailscale VPN (100% private, zero port-forwarding):

  • Step 1: Install the free 'Tailscale' app from Google Play Store or iOS App Store.
  • Step 2: Sign in with your registered Google account and toggle the connection switch ON.
  • Step 3: Open your mobile browser and go to:
http://100.80.199.101:8856

📸 Scorecard Photo & Screenshot OCR

1. Take Screenshot: Take a screenshot of your result scorecard on Testbook, Oliveboard, or Adda247.
2. Upload or Paste: Tap '📷 Upload Scorecard' to select the image, or simply press 'Ctrl + V' on computer.
3. Auto Calculation: Lakshya's smart scanner reads your marks, total questions, and automatically calculates negative penalties!
