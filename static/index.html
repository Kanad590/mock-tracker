Lakshya Setup & Mobile Browser Guide

Step-by-step instructions for Mobile Browser, PWA install, and Remote Access

🌐 1. Accessing on Mobile Browser

Open Google Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone. Type your server address in the browser bar. No heavy third-party app download is required.

📲 2. Install as Native App (1-Tap PWA)

🤖 Android (Google Chrome)

Android (Chrome): Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top-right corner -> Select 'Install app' or 'Add to Home screen'. A full-screen app icon will be pinned to your phone!

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Apple Safari)

iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button (⎋) at the bottom -> Scroll down and tap 'Add to Home Screen (+)' -> Tap 'Add'.

⚡ 3. Ultra-Fast Offline Caching

Lakshya operates with full service worker caching and offline SQLite persistence, opening in under 100ms even with patchy internet.

🏠 Connecting Phones on Home Wi-Fi (LAN)

Local Network

To open this tracker on your phone, tablet, or another family device connected to the same Wi-Fi router:

1. Find Your Host IP: • Linux / Pi: hostname -I
• Windows: ipconfig (IPv4 Address)
• macOS: System Settings > Network > Wi-Fi Details
2. Open on Phone: Type http://<HOST-IP>:8856 into Chrome or Safari on your phone.

🚀 Outside Access & Travel (Zero Port Forwarding)

Remote VPN

When away from home, connect securely using either Tailscale VPN or a secure reverse proxy:

Option A: Tailscale Mesh VPN (Recommended)

Install free Tailscale on your host and phone. Open your host's 100.x.y.z IP or MagicDNS hostname (e.g. http://my-host:8856) anywhere on 4G/5G mobile data with zero port exposure.

Option B: Cloudflare Tunnel / HTTPS Domain

Expose via Cloudflare Tunnel or Caddy/Nginx reverse proxy with a free Let's Encrypt SSL certificate for public domain access.
